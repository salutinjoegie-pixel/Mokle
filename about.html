Our Section

Section Mauchly is named after John William Mauchly (1907–1980) — an American physicist and computing pioneer who co-designed ENIAC (Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer) alongside J. Presper Eckert. ENIAC, completed in 1945, is widely regarded as one of the world's first general-purpose electronic computers. Like our namesake, we are a section driven by innovation, problem-solving, and a shared passion for technology.

Our Mission

To build a collaborative and inclusive learning community where every student is empowered to grow as a future IT professional — developing competencies in programming, networking, systems design, and beyond, while upholding a culture of academic excellence, integrity, and mutual respect.
